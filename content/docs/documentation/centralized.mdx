---
title: Centralized
description: These are the layers of the BRM related that are centrally owned / controlled
---

Every layer of the Blockchain Regulation Matrix has a **centralized** side:
the version of that layer operated by an identifiable company, consortium, or
institution — a utility supplying miners, an exchange holding customer
assets, a custodial staking service, a licensed stablecoin issuer.

Centralized operators are where regulation attaches most naturally, because
there is someone to license, examine, and hold accountable. That is why, in
the matrix's heat-map view, the most-regulated cells cluster on the
centralized side.

Explore the centralized column of each layer in the
[interactive matrix](/matrix) — click any left-hand icon to pin that cell's
full analysis, regulatory landscape, and incidents.
